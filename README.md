## 📌 Project Overview

**ClipKart Sales Performance Dashboard** is a data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI to present key business performance metrics in a clear and interactive format.

The dashboard provides insights into total revenue, order volume, product performance, category-wise revenue distribution, city-wise performance, and the relationship between product price and revenue.

The main objective is to transform sales data into meaningful visual insights that support business analysis and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance using KPI cards.
- Analyze revenue generated by different product categories.
- Identify top revenue-generating products.
- Compare sales performance across cities.
- Visualize the relationship between product price and revenue.
- Present business metrics through a clean, interactive dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue by Category
A column chart comparing revenue across Clothing, Electronics, and Groceries.

### 2. Top Revenue Products
A table highlighting leading products by revenue, including Phone, Bread, Jacket, Jeans, and T-Shirt.

### 3. Top Performing Cities
A bar chart comparing city-level sales revenue to help identify strong-performing markets.

### 4. Category Revenue Distribution
A doughnut chart showing each product category's share of total revenue.

### 5. Price vs Revenue Analysis
A line-style visual examining revenue patterns across product prices and highlighting variations in revenue.

## 💡 Business Insights

- Revenue is distributed across the three main product categories, allowing category-level performance comparisons.
- Phone and Bread are among the leading products by revenue in the displayed top-products table.
- City-wise comparisons help identify markets that may deserve further investigation.
- Price versus revenue analysis can help explore whether higher-priced products are associated with different revenue patterns.
- KPI cards provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

> **Note:** These are observations from the dashboard visuals. Further analysis of the underlying dataset is required to confirm causes, profitability, sales growth, and business trends.
